# Recording a trial observation

One page, for whoever is doing the recording. Send it with the link, or print
it for the ute.

The moment people get stuck is almost never inside the app — it is "what is
this link and what am I meant to do with it". That happens before the app is
open, so no amount of help inside it arrives in time.

---

## What you have been sent

A link, and a short code.

The link already knows which trial, which paddock, and which practice you are
recording. That is the point of it — you do not choose any of that, and you
cannot file a run against the wrong paddock by accident.

The code is typed once on each device and remembered afterwards.

## Doing it

1. **Tap the link.** It opens in your normal browser. Nothing to install.
2. **Type the code** if it asks. It will only ask the first time on that phone.
3. **Check the top of the screen.** It names the trial and the paddock. If that
   is not where you are standing, stop and ring whoever sent it.
4. **Fill in what it asks.** A few things at a time, with Next between them.
5. **Tap Save.**

That is the whole job.

## It works with no signal

This matters more than anything else on this page.

Your entry is saved on the phone the moment you tap Save, whether or not you
have bars. It sends itself when you are back in range — you do not have to
remember to do anything, and you do not have to stay in the paddock waiting for
it.

The line above the form tells you which is happening:

| What it says | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Connected — entries send as you save** | It has gone up. |
| **No signal — entries save on this device and send themselves when you are back in range** | Normal. Keep recording. |
| **⚠ … could not be sent** | Something is actually wrong. See below. |

The middle one is not an error. A paddock with no reception is the expected
case and the app is built for it.

## If you see the red line

"… could not be sent and is still on this device only."

Nothing is lost — the entry is on the phone. But it will not go up on its own,
and somebody needs to know.

**Tell whoever set the trial up, and read them what it says.** The dashboard
shows the exact reason. Do not re-enter the data; it is already there.

## Photos and videos

If the form asks for one, the button opens the camera straight away. Take it
and carry on — it uploads with everything else when there is signal.

Keep videos under about a minute. A long clip from a paddock is a long upload.

## If you get something wrong

Under the form is **Already recorded here** — the last few entries at this
paddock, from everyone, not just your phone.

Two things that is for: checking your run went in, and noticing you have
already done plot 7. Each row has **Fix** if a number is wrong.

## Questions people actually ask

**Do I need an account?** No. The link and the code are all there is.

**Can I use my own phone?** Yes. That is the expectation.

**What if two of us record the same plot?** Both entries are kept. Nothing is
overwritten, and the duplicate can be sorted out afterwards — better than
somebody deciding not to record because they were unsure.

**Do I need to be online to start?** Only the first time you open the link on
that phone. After that it opens with no signal.

**Does it use much data?** No. Text entries are tiny. Photos and videos are the
only meaningful part, and they wait for a connection.

**Can I close it halfway through?** Save first if you can. A part-filled form
that has not been saved is not kept.

crop with one hand free.
